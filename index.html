Authenticated Health Sources & Medical Guidelines

Every recommendation aligns strictly with international standards from WHO, CDC, and the American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM).

🏛️

World Health Organization (WHO)

Recommends at least 150–300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity weekly or 75–150 minutes of vigorous training, plus muscle-strengthening activities 2+ days weekly. Dietary guidelines recommend limiting free sugars to <5-10% and sodium to <2g/day (5g salt).

Read WHO Nutrition Fact Sheet →
🏥

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

Recommends the "Healthy Plate Method" (1/2 non-starchy vegetables & fruits, 1/4 whole grains, 1/4 lean protein). Safe sustainable weight regulation targets 0.5–1.0 kg (1–2 lbs) per week through gradual caloric adjustments rather than extreme deprivation.

CDC Healthy Weight & Food →
🏋️

American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)

Formulates standard push-up and calisthenic progressions: Incline/Knee push-ups for beginners (elevating load smoothly from 9% to ~55%), standard floor push-ups lifting ~64% of bodyweight, and decline/plyometric push-ups for progressive hypertrophy.
